AmbientSenseBench

Synthetic ambient-sensing data generator for privacy-preserving home-routine monitoring

Scenarios model changes in daily routine studied as a behavioural proxy in mental-health research. They are synthetic data generated from explicit assumptions — not clinical data, diagnostic labels, or evidence of performance with real residents.
